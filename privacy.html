Overview

Tanakh to Testament is designed to provide a Bible reading plan and progress-tracking experience with as little personal-data collection as possible.

Information we collect

Tanakh to Testament does not require you to create an account. The developer does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information through the app.

Reading plan and progress data

Your reading-plan settings and reading progress are intended to remain on your device unless a future version of the app clearly introduces an optional synchronization or account feature. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated.

Third-party and platform services

The app may rely on standard services provided by Apple or the app's technical framework to operate, distribute updates, or diagnose technical issues. Those services may process technical information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Tanakh to Testament does not use personal information for advertising.

Children's privacy

Tanakh to Testament is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when the policy is updated.
