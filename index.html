About the app

Tanakh to Testament helps readers move through the Old Testament in the traditional Jewish threefold order—Torah, Prophets, and Writings—before continuing into the New Testament.

Choose the number of days for your plan, follow your daily Scripture references, and track your progress through the whole Bible.
